The strategic opportunity
The vision

The consent form is already at the center of the patient journey.

Today, Faces owns the operational touchpoint.

Patient information, medical history, consents, documentation, payments and clinic workflows.

Crisalix adds the visual intelligence layer.

3D preview, facial-area prioritisation, patient concerns and treatment-opportunity discovery.

Together, it becomes more than consent.

A communication platform, education platform and AI recommendation engine.

The result: a growth engine for clinics.

Better consultations, more trust, stronger conversion and repeat engagement.

From administration to intelligence.The prototype demonstrates how a required consent workflow can become the first step in a smarter patient relationship.
